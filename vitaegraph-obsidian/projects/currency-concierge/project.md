# Currency Concierge

## Executive summary

Currency Concierge is described as a hotel currency-exchange service concept with compliance orientation, authorized exchange-operator partnerships, API integration, multilingual receipts, exchange-rate calculation, margin handling, and operation logging.

## Problem, users, and constraints

Hotels serving international guests often face currency, payment, and guest-service questions. This concept explores how a hotel-facing service could support exchange-rate calculation, receipts, and internal logs while respecting Italian compliance requirements and authorized operator boundaries.
