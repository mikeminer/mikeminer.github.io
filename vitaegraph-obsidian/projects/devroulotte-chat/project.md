# DevRoulotte.chat

## Executive summary

DevRoulotte.chat is described as a vertical speed-networking platform for the tech world, connecting developers, founders, investors, recruiters, designers, and builders through timed 1:1 video matches.

Live link: https://devroulotte.chat

## Problem, users, and constraints

The project addresses professional discovery and rapid matching in builder ecosystems. The intended users are Italian developers, founders, builders, makers, recruiters, investors, and adjacent product/design profiles who benefit from short, structured conversations.

## Personal role and ownership

As CEO of DevRoulotte, Michele is positioned as the product owner and public builder. Available sources support concept design, product communication, profile-card ideas, business-plan material, and community validation loops.

## Architecture and product model

The available material describes:

- 1:1 timed video matching.
- Free basic matching, chat, and essential filters.
- Premium concepts such as longer calls, stack/role filters, and priority queue.
- Lightweight profile cards and LinkedIn login concepts.
