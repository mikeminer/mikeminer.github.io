About

From front desk pressure to operating systems.

My work starts in real operations: night audit, guest reception, payments, daily accounting close, reporting, PMS checks and the pressure of keeping a hotel running while most people are offline.

The common thread across the lab is operational clarity. I use AI, scripts, APIs, local tools and onchain primitives to make hospitality, trading and builder workflows easier to read, automate and iterate.

I am especially interested in the edge between human coordination and autonomous systems: where agents, dashboards, wallets, PMS workflows and data pipelines become practical instruments rather than demos.

I am also an active member of the ItalianBuilders community, contributing to open-source software with other Italian builders. Current community work includes Magistra, an early-stage legal AI knowledge graph for Italian law.
