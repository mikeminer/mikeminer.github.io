About

From signal to system.

My work starts before code: framing the problem, identifying the moving parts, then shaping the smallest useful system that can be tested in the real world.

The common thread across the lab is operational clarity. I use AI, scripts, APIs, local tools and onchain primitives to make complex flows easier to read, automate and iterate.

I am especially interested in the edge between human coordination and autonomous systems: where agents, dashboards, wallets and data pipelines become practical instruments rather than demos.
